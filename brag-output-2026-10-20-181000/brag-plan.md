# Brag Plan: OpenCell

## What is this app?
OpenCell is an open-source Android Phone and SMS app with a unified call list, local computer gateway, and permission-first personal assistant.

## The angle
Treat the phone like infrastructure that finally belongs to its owner: one calm, green control surface for calls, messages, memory, voice, and approvals. The joke is understated—this is a phone app that refuses to behave like a black box.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Large type lands over the project’s green phone mockup: “Your phone. Your assistant.” A compact call/message/assistant stack snaps into place underneath.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real website assistant mockup types a Hinglish preference and returns “I’ll remember that.”
- Three product capabilities arrive one by one: one call list, local gateway, permission first.
- The voice conversation affordance appears as a phone UI card, grounded by the actual Assistant screen label “Start voice conversation”.

## Outro / punchline
“One app. Your rules.” resolves to the OpenCell mark and the live demo URL.

## User flow worth showing
Open the Assistant → enter a preference or reminder → review the assistant’s response and memory/action boundary. This is represented with fictional stand-ins only; no personal phone data is used.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet premium product film for an owner-controlled phone
- Interpretation: restrained motion, generous holds, crisp green accents, and no claims beyond the project’s own README and website copy.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f7f8f5`
- Accent: `#0f5132`
- Text: `#102019`
- Display font: system sans-serif, matching `website/index.html`
- Body font: system sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: the website’s rounded green phone card and assistant chat bubbles.

## Share copy (draft)
OpenCell: your phone, your assistant, and your rules—calls, SMS, gateway, memory, and approvals in one open-source Android app.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, lightly rhythmic product bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: soft entrance, restrained bed under readable copy, fade beneath the final logo.
- Music cue guidance: use the bundled cue metadata at composition time; target strong reveals near 4.2s, 9.8s, and 16.6s, while preserving readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the green hero glow breathe with the music, never use waveform graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched; one soft key/tap for the assistant entry, gentle ticks for capability cards, one final logo hit.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed assistant line, sequential capability cards, final mark landing.
- Restraint rule: no narration, no real phone audio, and no distracting notification sounds.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Owner mode — 3.0s
Green phone card and the exact site headline “Your phone. Your assistant.” appear with a clean, confident reveal.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: warm opening swell
Audio-coupled idea: soft title hit
Transition mood: polished → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Assistant in use — 5.0s
Recreate the website’s assistant panel. A fictional user preference types in, followed by “I’ll remember that.” A small caption says “Memory stays under your control.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — type the preference, then reveal the response bubble.
Audio intent: human, useful, calm
Audio-coupled idea: subtle typing ticks and response chime
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The phone stack — 5.0s
Three rounded cards arrive one by one: “One call list”, “Phone-native SMS”, and “Local gateway”. Use the exact feature names from the website.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards reveal in order and hold together.
Audio intent: light rhythmic pulse
Audio-coupled idea: restrained card ticks aligned to the beat grid
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Permission first — 4.0s
Show a compact assistant approval card with “External actions are previewed before they run.” Then show the actual voice affordance label “Start voice conversation”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — approval card, then voice card.
Audio intent: confidence and safety
Audio-coupled idea: one low confirmation tone per reveal
Transition mood: dramatic but quiet → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Your rules — 3.0s
OpenCell mark, “One app. Your rules.” and the live demo URL settle on the green background.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: final soft logo hit and fade
Audio-coupled idea: logo landing
Transition mood: clean end
