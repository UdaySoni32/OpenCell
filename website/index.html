One app, your rules

Communication without the lock-in.

OpenCell keeps the phone number and core data on your device. Connect your own AI provider, your own computer, or nothing else.

☎

One call list

SIM calls and supported app-call notifications appear together, with callback paths for each source.

✉

Phone-native SMS

Use OpenCell as the default SMS app for threads, delivery state, notifications, and direct sending.

⌘

Local gateway

Pair a computer over Wi-Fi, USB, or Tailscale. Use the CLI, Python library, or MCP server.

✦

Bring your own AI

Use Ollama, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, or the optional speech server.

✓

Permission first

Calls and messages pass through one policy with approvals, scopes, a kill switch, and an audit log.

◌

Voice modes

Use Shizuku or speakerphone modes for AI-assisted calls, with text fallback when audio is unavailable.

Personal assistant

Memory that stays under your control.

Tell OpenCell what matters. Explicit memories are saved immediately; inferred memories remain reviewable until you confirm them. Delete or edit anything from the app.

Start in minutes

Install it on the phone you already have.

No OpenCell account, VoIP number, or OpenCell cloud is required.

1

Install the APK

Download the latest release and make OpenCell the default Phone and SMS app during the guided setup.

2

Choose your AI

Configure your own provider in Settings. Keys are stored with Android Keystore encryption.

3

Pair your computer

Enable Computers & API, pair with a six-digit code, then use opencell from any supported OS.

Privacy note: call recording and learning from calls are opt-in. Laws about AI-assisted calls and recording vary by location.
